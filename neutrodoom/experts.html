Neutrophil research in Brazil

Meet five researchers exploring the biology of neutrophils, from infection to inflammation. Follow their work on Google Scholar.

  • Claudia Ida Brodskyn

    Investigates neutrophils in leishmaniasis, including their interactions with macrophages in parasite control and their involvement in inflammation and tissue damage.

    Google Scholar↗

  • Elvira Saraiva

    Investigates how neutrophils release extracellular traps (NETs) against Leishmania and how these parasites resist or escape this defense.

    Google Scholar↗

  • Fernando de Queiroz Cunha

    Investigates how neutrophil migration and neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs) influence infection control and organ damage in sepsis.

    Google Scholar↗

  • Marco Aurélio Ramirez Vinolo

    Studies how microbiota-derived metabolites, especially short-chain fatty acids, regulate neutrophil migration and activity in inflammation and defense against infection.

    Google Scholar↗

  • Sandra Helena Poliselli Farsky

    Studies how neutrophils develop, migrate and respond to inflammation, with a focus on annexin A1 and the effects of toxic exposures.

    Google Scholar↗
