HOW SCORING WORKS

Elimination: 100 points. Engulf: 150. Chain kills within 4.5 seconds: ×2 at 3, ×3 at 6, ×4 at 9. Each cleared wave: +500. Victory: +2,000, plus up to 2,000 for tissue and 500 per unused reinforcement. Collateral damage: −15 points per damage point.

Rating: campaign 600 + tissue 250 + collateral control 100 + survival 50. Quality components scale with completed waves, so an early defeat cannot earn a high grade. This is a game rubric, not a percentile or a scientific measure.

THE BIOLOGY

Inspired by a tissue slide. Pink and purple come from hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) staining. They are an artistic choice, not the natural colors of living organisms.

Oxidation in two places. The F ability represents reactive oxygen species (ROS) released outside the cell, which can also damage tissue. ROS also act inside the phagosome to help destroy engulfed microorganisms.

Capsules and opsonization. Capsules hinder phagocytosis. Antibodies and complement components can tag invaders and aid recognition. The Y pickup symbolizes this help.

Size changes the response. Large hyphae resist complete engulfment. Target shape and size influence neutrophil responses, including NET release.

Game abstractions. Digestion restoring attack resources and every defeat producing a NET are game abstractions. In biology, not all neutrophil deaths involve NETs, and NET release can occur without immediate lysis.

Papers: target size and NETs · NETs without immediate lysis.
